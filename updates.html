Update 1.2

THE NEHEMIAH PACK, PT. 1

It is with great pleasure to announce the release of the Nehemiah Pack, featuring new songs and exclusive content!

Here's what you can expect from the Nehemiah Pack:

  • 4 new songs; These songs include:
    • "NEHEMIAH", a song about Ebanks's current life status with help from a very special guest.
    • "Farm on the Hill", from The Great Cow Father featuring Ebanks. It's taken some time since we announced it would be coming as we had to make some last-minute changes.
    • "It Seems Like You're Ready", a cover of the original song by R. Kelly. This is a cover that holds special meaning to Ebanks, and he was happy to let us include this.
    • "INTERNET", a song originally intended for Ebanks's sophomore album but was cut due to time constraints.
  • Exclusive content, such as a special video for each song in the pack and transcripts from interviews with the artists involved in the pack.

We are very excited to share this pack with you and hope you enjoy the new songs and exclusive content!

Though, there is one thing...

What is it?

We aren't quite done with Farm On The Hill and INTERNET yet, they still need some finishing touches. NEHEMIAH and It Seems Like You're Ready, however, are complete and ready.

They'll be available in the songs section of our website. When the rest of the songs are finished, we'll update the website with a link to the mediafire page for the Nehemiah pack.

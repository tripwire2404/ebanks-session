About The Website & The Session

Welcome! This website celebrates an incredible collaborative session between Ebanks and an award-winning artist. It's dedicated to sharing information, updates, and music from this legendary 6.5-hour creative marathon.

MAIN IDEA

What is the session and why is it so important?

The session was an epic collaborative musical event featuring Ebanks and a renowned guest artist, complete with additional guest singers and rappers joining in. Talk about an all-star lineup!

The significance? Over 150 songs came out of this session! Two creative minds came together and produced an absolutely massive amount of material. That's basically an entire discography in one afternoon.

Why haven't these songs appeared on Ebanks' records?

Good question! Due to licensing and contractual agreements, Ebanks is legally unable to release these songs for an undisclosed period of time. It's one of those mysterious music industry situations—kind of like how unreleased albums from the 80s occasionally pop up decades later!

Honestly, we're not even sure Ebanks will remember half of what happened that day. Six and a half hours is a LONG session. 😄

Why Do All Of This?

Initially, I planned to keep these songs private after receiving them from a colleague with connections to the session archives. But then I realized—why keep this gold locked away? These tracks deserve to be heard! I decided to create this archive as a tribute to an absolutely legendary creative session.

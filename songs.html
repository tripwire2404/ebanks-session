THE SONGS

1. Satisfy You (Predator Mix)

This song was a collaboration between Ebanks and 2 special artists. They all came together to create a remix to a song that still holds up to this day.

2. Bump & Grind (Ebanks Mix)

This song features Ebanks taking on a classic hit "Bump & Grind" with a fresh new twist. The track combines sensual lyrics with a modern beat, showcasing Ebanks' unique style.

That's right, the special guest was Sampha! He was more than happy to come in at midnight to help Ebanks with this.

Video for NEHEMIAH (feat. Sampha)

ANOTHER P.S. Ebanks sounds different on the first verse because he decided to take a verse he recorded when he was younger. The 2nd verse is the one that was recorded during the session.

2. Farm On The Hill

This song is a more laid-back track that showcases Ebanks' versatility as an artist while assisting The Great Cow Father with smooth vocals and a chill beat. "Farm On The Hill" needed a few changes since we announced the release of the song in a very different way than originally intended.

Video for Farm On The Hill

3. It Seems Like You're Ready

A cover of the song from R. Kelly's catalogue (for some reason), "It Seems Like You're Ready" has Ebanks do it in his own way. Ebanks has claimed the original song is one of his favorites, and he has said that out of all the songs recorded in the session, this is probably the one he is the most proud of.

Video for It Seems Like You're Ready

4. INTERNET

This isn't a song that was a part of the original session, but rather a track made before then with the intent of being on Ebanks's 2nd album. It could not make the cut due to time constraints, and copies of the CD released in the UK that has the track as a bonus is hard to come by. Thankfully, we knew a guy who had a copy and shared it with us.

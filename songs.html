1. Satisfy You (Predator Mix)

This song was a collaboration between Ebanks, R. Kelly, and Diddy. They all came together to create a remix to a song that hasn't aged so well due to recent circumstances.

2. Bump & Grind (Ebanks Mix)

This song features Ebanks taking on R. Kelly's classic hit "Bump & Grind" with a fresh new twist. The track combines sensual lyrics with a modern beat, showcasing Ebanks' unique style.
